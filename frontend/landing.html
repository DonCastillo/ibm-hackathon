## Features

Everything your team shipped. Nothing it didn't.

Reads the code, not the commit message

Summaries come from the diffs, so “fix stuff” still becomes a real update.

Accomplishments only

Formatting, spacing, reverts and minor tweaks are filtered out automatically.

Every branch

Unmerged feature-branch work is included, so nothing is missed.

3 formats × 2 audiences

Slack, Email and Standup, for developers or clients, all generated at once.

Private repos

Works with a Personal Access Token, used only to clone and never stored.

Who shipped what

Per-developer highlights and branches at a glance.

## Why it's different

Not another changelog bot.

changelog-bot.md ↔ standup-sync.md
-Lists commit messages as-is: “wip”, “fix stuff”, “update”
+Reads each diff and says what actually changed
-Every commit counts, including reverts and formatting
+Filters the noise and reports major updates only
-Only sees the default branch
+Covers every branch, including unmerged work
-One technical format for everyone
+Writes for your audience: developers or clients, in Slack, Email or Standup form

## How it works

From git log to a finished update in five steps.

  1. no LLM

    Git repo

    Commits from every branch in your date range.

  2. no LLM

    Noise filter

    Drops merges, lock files, spacing-only changes and reverts.

  3. LLM

    Reads the diffs

    One short summary per commit, tagged MAJOR or MINOR.

  4. no LLM

    Highlights

    Major updates only, grouped by area and author.

  5. LLM

    Outputs

    Slack · Email · Standup, for developers and for clients.

The LLM only runs on commits that survive the filter. The exact rules are in filtering.md.
